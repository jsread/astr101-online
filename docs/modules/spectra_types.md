### Earthshine

We see the moon and planets in reflected light from the sun

We can see the darker part of a crescent moon from "Earthshine": sunlight light reflected from the Earth

![When the Moon appears as a thin crescent in the twilight skies of Earth it is often possible to see that the rest of the disc is also faintly glowing. This phenomenon is called earthshine. It is due to sunlight reflecting off the Earth and illuminating the lunar surface. After reflection from Earth the colours in the light, shown as a rainbow in this picture, are significantly changed](img/eso1210c.jpg)
